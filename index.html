How to play

Guess the word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a real word.

After each guess, the tiles show how close you were:

WORDY

W is in the word, in the right spot.

LIGHT

I is in the word, but elsewhere.

ROGUE

U is not in the word at all.

Daily is one shared word per day. Random and 6-Letter are unlimited; play as much as you like.
