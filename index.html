How To Play

Guess the word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a real word.

After each guess, the tiles change colour to show how close you were:

WORDY

W is in the word and in the right spot.

LIGHT

I is in the word but in the wrong spot.

ROGUE

U is not in the word at all.

Modes: Daily is the same word for everyone, once a day. Random and 6-Letter are unlimited — hit Play Again as much as you like.

Settings

  • Hard Mode Revealed hints must be used in later guesses.
  • High Contrast Colour-blind friendly tiles (orange / blue).
  • Sound Play a pop when you reveal a correct letter.
